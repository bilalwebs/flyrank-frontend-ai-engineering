# NICE_TO_HAVE.md — Task 04: Survive the Crit

Improvements that increase polish but are not hiring blockers.

---

| # | Suggestion | Benefit | Priority |
|---|-----------|---------|----------|
| NH1 | **Project screenshots / images** | Visual proof of work. A screenshot of the running app is worth 1000 words of description. | High |
| NH2 | **Resume download link** | Hiring managers expect a downloadable PDF resume. Many companies require it for their records. | High |
| NH3 | **Testimonials or recommendations** | Social proof from mentors, colleagues, or internship supervisors builds trust. | Medium |
| NH4 | **Blog or writing section** | Writing about what you learn demonstrates depth of understanding and communication skills. | Medium |
| NH5 | **Custom 404 page** | Shows attention to detail and UX thinking. A 404 page is a portfolio touchpoint. | Low |
| NH6 | **Dark/light mode toggle** | Demonstrates CSS custom properties knowledge and user preference handling. | Low |
| NH7 | **Unique visual identity** | The purple/blue gradient on dark background is the most overused developer portfolio palette. A unique color scheme or visual style would make the portfolio memorable. | Medium |
| NH8 | **Animation variety** | Every section uses the same `opacity: 0 → 1, y: 20 → 0` entrance. Varied animations (slide-in, stagger, parallax) would feel more dynamic. | Low |
| NH9 | **Education section** | For an intern, education (university, degree, GPA, relevant coursework) is expected and provides context. | Medium |
| NH10 | **OG / social preview image** | A custom Open Graph image makes shared links look professional on LinkedIn, Twitter, and Discord. | Low |
| NH11 | **Page transition animations** | Smooth transitions between scroll sections would improve the sense of flow. | Low |
| NH12 | **Micro-interactions on cards** | Hover effects, subtle transforms, or click feedback on project cards would feel more polished. | Low |
| NH13 | **Year of experience with dates** | "2024–Present" is more credible than "3+ yrs." Specific dates signal honesty. | Medium |
| NH14 | **Loading skeleton** | A skeleton loader instead of blank content during page load feels more professional. | Low |
| NH15 | **Analytics / tracking** | Knowing which sections get the most attention helps optimize the portfolio over time. | Low |

---

## Priority Legend

- **High** — Would noticeably improve hiring impression if added
- **Medium** — Nice polish, shows extra effort
- **Low** — Cosmetic, only worth it if everything else is done

---

*These are recommendations for future iterations, not required for Task-04 completion.*
