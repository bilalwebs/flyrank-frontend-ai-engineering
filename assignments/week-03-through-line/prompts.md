## Reflection

The prompts followed the Prompt Ladder technique from Week 2:
- **Version 1:** Broad analysis of the existing portfolio structure.
- **Version 2:** Focused generation of the four required sections (Claim, Content Map, CTA Hierarchy, Proof to Gather).
- **Version 3:** Review pass to catch gaps and ensure alignment with the primary goal.

Each prompt added a specific constraint without changing everything at once, making it easy to see what each iteration improved.
