## Assignment Overview

This assignment explores different technology stacks for building a professional portfolio website.

The goal is to evaluate multiple options, compare their trade-offs, and choose the most suitable stack based on personal skills, project requirements, maintainability, and future scalability.

---

## Deliverables

- Stack analysis
- Three technology stack options
- Trade-off comparison
- Pressure testing
- Final technology decision
- Personal rationale

After evaluating three different approaches, I selected **Next.js 16 + React 19 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS** because it best aligns with my current skill level, internship requirements, and long-term portfolio goals.
